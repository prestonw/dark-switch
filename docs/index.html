Dark Switch

Automatically switch between light and dark mode
based on the app you are using.

  • Home
  • Privacy Policy

What it does

Dark Switch sits in your menu bar and watches which application is in the foreground. When you switch apps, it automatically sets macOS to light or dark mode based on the rules you configure. No scheduled timers, no cloud sync — just instant, per-app appearance control.

Open source

Dark Switch is free, open source software released under the MIT License. You are free to inspect, modify, and distribute the code.

Privacy

Dark Switch does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All configuration is saved locally on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
